# Claim check｜宣稱清查（student version）

Short rule for article and page text on a student site. The long method, with its sources and limits, lives in the maintainers' workbench as the `claim-check` skill; this file is the version the student's AI can follow without it.

## What it checks
Whether the source the text points to really says what the text says. It does not prove the source is right, and it does not check the student's own experience.

## Steps
1. **List the claims** that would need proof if someone asked: numbers, rules and policies, "the best / first / latest", medical, legal, financial and safety statements, product features, "currently". Skip the student's own opinions and experiences.
2. **Open the source yourself in this session.** Memory, search snippets and "it probably says" do not count. If the source is only a recap, go up to the original report, official page or dataset.
3. **Grade the source.**
   - Prefer: official documents, government and regulator pages, universities, original datasets, studies with a stated method.
   - Acceptable only when nothing better exists: reputable reporting that links to its original.
   - Do not accept: content-farm articles, unsourced round-ups, affiliate lists, pages with no trail back to a source.
   - Several pages repeating one study are one source.
4. **Compare** number, wording, place, method and period. A statement that is newer, bigger or broader than the source is a mismatch.
5. **Record a verdict per claim** with the evidence: the final URL, the date you looked, and a short quote of the matching passage.

## Verdicts (no other words)
| Verdict | Meaning |
|---|---|
| PASS | Opened this session; a good source says it in matching context (a faithful paraphrase is fine) |
| FAIL | The source does not say it, says the opposite, a different place or period, or is a source we do not accept |
| BLOCKED | Could not open or read it (tool, network, paywall, script-only page) |
| NOT TESTED | No source given, only a summary without the passage was available, or not checked this time |

## High-risk statements
Medical effect, legal or compliance, money and tax, safety and crisis handling, testimonials, credentials and licences: with no good source they do **not stay in the text**. Fix = add a source or delete. Never invent a source, a credential or a quote to make a claim pass.

## Report
Counts per verdict, one table row per claim (claim, source, verdict, evidence, fix), and a list of what could not be checked (images, paywalls, the student's first-hand experience). The report edits nothing. Whether to publish is the student's decision.
